Part 1 · How AI changed

In 2026, AI went from helper to worker.

You give it a task. It reads the project, makes a plan, writes the code, tests it, and asks you to review.

booking-appAI agent working
$ agent "Let users book by phone number" ● Reading the project… (214 files) ● Plan: 4 steps ✎ Editing booking.js, api.js, form.tsx… ✓ 48 tests passed → Ready for your review
(~1.5 min) Let it type. In 2025 most tools suggested the next line. In 2026 "agents" take a whole task: read the code base, plan, edit many files, run the tests, and hand back a finished change for review. Ask: "Who has used an AI agent like this?"

What you learn in Japan

Clients don't say everything. Learn to notice.

Japan is a high-context culture. Working there, you learn to sense what clients really want, and deliver it with care.

★ Japan Quality Care in every detail
WHAT THEY SAY"Please make a booking app."
WHAT THEY REALLY WANT
Easy for older customers
No mistakes on busy days
Quick help when stuck
(~2 min) AI cannot talk with your clients, and clients rarely say everything they want. Japan is a high-context culture: people expect you to understand what is not said. The Japanese word is 察する, sassuru. Example: the client only says "Please make a booking app." Under the surface they want it to be easy for older customers, to work without mistakes on busy days, and to get quick help when stuck. Working in Japan trains you to notice (気づく), think ahead (先回り) and care about every detail (心配り). Click: that is Japan Quality, service that clients trust and come back for.

The lesson

Good questions find what the client didn't say.

Ask why.

What problem should this solve? Why now?

“What should change after the app launches?”
Ask who and when.

Picture one real user on one real day.

“Who opens the app first, and where are they?”
Ask how we'll know.

Agree on what success looks like.

“How will you measure ‘safer’?”

kakunin · confirm. Then show a simple sketch early and check again. In Japan, confirming is a sign of care, not of weakness.

(~1.5 min) Three habits: ask why (purpose), ask who and when (the real situation), ask how we'll know it worked (success criteria). Click: then confirm. Show a rough sketch early and ask "Is this what you mean?" Japanese clients appreciate this (確認, 報連相). This is what AI cannot do for you, and what makes you the person the AI works for.
